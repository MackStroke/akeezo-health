# Decisions, assumptions and missing inputs

## Established scope

The requested deliverable is a Markdown specification pack for a future MERN application. It is not an implementation, deployment or migration. The reference is CureMeAbroad; the target brand is Akeezo. Akeezo's own site supplies the observed teal/orange/Lato theme. MongoDB is the database; React, Express and Node are the application stack.

“Exact” is interpreted as matching the reference's section order, page families, relative geometry, interactions and visual density while applying Akeezo branding. Different logos, fonts, copy lengths and approved content will change pixel output. Final parity requires later captures at matching viewports and approved tolerances.

## Implementation defaults

- Keep established Akeezo canonical routes such as `/about`, `/blog`, `/faq`, `/privacy` and `/terms`; map reference equivalents through aliases.
- English is the base locale. Russian and Uzbek are proposed reviewed translations, not autogenerated launch content.
- India is the initial provider/destination focus; additional destinations require real service coverage and content.
- Use SSR for public discovery and private client interfaces for operational tools. Exact RouteEntry registration resolves ambiguous SEO paths.
- Keep optional community, account, partner subscription and third-party service integrations behind explicit feature gates until their operations exist.
- No checkout, paid subscription engine, clinical diagnosis or emergency dispatch capability is implied by the baseline.
- Build privately with synthetic fixtures; public business data must be approved. Fixture records must never ship as real providers, reviews or outcomes.

## Inputs and release gates

These are tasks for the implementation owner, not reasons to stop writing or scaffolding unrelated modules.

| Input | Why it matters | Safe interim behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved official SVG logo files and asset rights | Final branding and lawful media use | Validate documented official URLs; use controlled local copies |
| Akeezo legal entity, office locations and service scope | Footer, contact and legal pages | Keep unconfirmed fields unpublished |
| Partner inbox selection | Current site shows `partners@` and `partnership@` variants | Configurable draft setting; owner verifies intended inbox |
| Provider/doctor data and relationship proof | Accurate directory listings and credentials | Private synthetic fixtures; no implied affiliation |
| Current price bands, currency, inclusions and validity | Honest calculator output | Quote-request fallback without fabricated amounts |
| Real reviews/outcome media and publication consent | Trust and before-after modules | Hide module or show a neutral explanation |
| Team, reviewers and clinical content approval | Medical article accountability | Keep draft until qualified review |
| Partner categories, offer definitions and fees | Partner comparison and CTAs | Benefits-only draft; no invented subscriptions |
| Existing Akeezo route export and approved redirects | Prevent migration traffic loss | Preserve observed routes; audit before cutover |
| RU/UZ translations and locale operations | Readable, accurate international content | English only; hide unreviewed translations |
| Coordinator and moderation staffing | Real responses and community safety | Disable unsupported public workflows |
| Hosting, MongoDB, storage and mail configuration | Delivery, private records and recovery | Local adapters/test accounts in private environments |
| Privacy/consent/retention/legal approval | Handling sensitive enquiries | Minimize collection; no public production launch of unapproved flows |
| Analytics and cookie requirements | Consent-aware measurement | Essential operational telemetry only |

## Decisions to record during implementation

Pin supported dependency versions; select hosting regions and object storage; choose identity/session tooling; decide whether public HTML caching is needed; confirm operational response times; define price update ownership; set actual content retention rules; approve screenshot comparison tolerances. Keep these in repository ADRs and configuration, not ad hoc hardcoded component values.

## Completion truthfulness

This pack verifies documentation structure and its included URL inventory. It does not verify a running MERN application. Future completion reports must state tests actually run, unresolved integrations and disabled modules. Public rollout is a separate owner decision after private staging is reviewable.
