## Content migration rules

Source provider facts from approved Akeezo records and provider-authorized materials. Competitor listings are reference examples, not an Akeezo partner roster. Prices need written source, inclusions, exclusions, currency, date and expiry. Ratings need provenance and reuse permission. Team/press sections need real evidence. Medical guides need a named qualified reviewer and sources before publication.

Free-service language must specify the exact free service; prior campaign positioning does not imply hospital care, tests, travel or every Akeezo service is free. Do not copy competitor partner fees or discounts. Emergency messaging must not imply guaranteed dispatch or immediate clinical response without operational evidence; give a clear route to local emergency services when appropriate and keep the coordination form's role explicit.

## Initial taxonomy proposal

Priority categories: oncology, cardiology, orthopaedics, neurology, fertility, gastroenterology and urology, subject to actual partner coverage. Names may retain existing source slugs with aliases (e.g. cancer-treatment/oncology, orthopedics/orthopaedics). Transplantation and reproductive-service pages require specific provider/legal eligibility review before public offering; their presence in the reference is not authorization to promote every procedure.

India locations shown on current Akeezo home include Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Kochi, Goa, Jaipur and Pune. Treat displayed city names as migration candidates, not verified availability for every procedure.

## Localization

English canonical baseline; reviewed Russian and Uzbek locale variants. Store each translation as an independently reviewed revision associated through localeGroupId. Translation state draft/review/published. Never mix machine-translated medical advice into a published page silently. Use ISO language tags en, ru and uz; support Uzbek Latin characters. A locale switch stays on the equivalent route when translated; otherwise show an explicit English fallback without falsely tagging it as localized.

Use `Intl` for date/number/currency display. Costs preserve original quote currency and show conversion source/time when available. Allow 30–40% text expansion in buttons and headings. Validate names without English-only patterns. Consent and error messages must be localized consistently with the form.
